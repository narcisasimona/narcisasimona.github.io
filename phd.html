Doctoral research

Electrical Engineering · National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest

Proposed thesis title

Dezvoltarea Metodelor EEG pentru Cuantificarea și Antrenarea Atenției la Copiii cu Tulburări de Neurodezvoltare prin Activități Interactiv-Gamificate

Development of EEG Methods for Quantifying and Training Attention in Children with Neurodevelopmental Disorders through Interactive Game-Based Activities

I explore how electroencephalography (EEG) and performance during interactive game-based tasks can help identify markers of attention in children with ASD or ADHD. The scope and methodology may evolve as the research progresses.

Research direction

EEG and task design

Review suitable non-invasive EEG systems and plan age-appropriate interactive tasks that engage different aspects of attention.

Attention markers

Investigate how features extracted from EEG relate to behavioural measures such as accuracy and response time during the tasks.

Future applications

Explore whether these markers could inform the design of personalised attention-training activities.
